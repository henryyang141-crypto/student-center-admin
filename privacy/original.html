5. Jupiter 成绩：怎么运作，有哪些风险

这个版本怎么做

  • 你点“连接 Jupiter 自动同步成绩”，在 Clever / NYCENET 自己的登录页上输入账号密码。App 的代码不读取密码。
  • 登录成功后，App 把 Jupiter、Clever、NYCENET 的登录 Cookie 复制一份存进 iOS 钥匙串，这样 App 被关掉后也不用重新登录。
  • 之后 App 会在一个看不见的内置浏览器里打开 Jupiter，逐门课读取成绩和作业明细：打开 App 时（距离上次至少 10 分钟）、下拉刷新、点“立即同步”时，以及 iOS 安排的后台刷新（大约每 30 分钟最多一次，具体由 iOS 决定）。
  • 如果登录过期，App 会重新打开 Clever 的入口，并自动点一下 Clever 页面上的“NYCENET”登录按钮，让还有效的学校登录自动接上。它不会输入密码；如果需要密码，App 会停下来并通知你自己登录。
  • 出现新成绩或分数变化时，App 在手机本地发通知，通知里会显示课程名、作业名和分数。

风险点（请认真看）

  • 没有官方授权。Jupiter 没有给学生用的公开接口，这个 App 是读取你能看到的网页。Jupiter、Clever 或 NYC DOE 的使用条款、学校的电脑使用规定（Acceptable Use Policy）可能不允许用自动化工具访问。如果他们认为你违规，可能限制访问、锁定账号或要求你说明情况。
  • 可能被当成机器人。每次同步都会连续打开你所有课的页面。频繁同步、反复点按，或很多学生同时用，会产生异常流量，可能触发网站的限速或安全检查。
  • 成绩可能不准。Jupiter 一改网页，读取就可能失败、漏掉课程或读错数字。App 里的 GPA 是按 App 自己的规则估算的，不是官方成绩单，不能用来申诉或申请。
  • 浏览器标识。为了拿到电脑版成绩表，App 会以“Mac 上的 Safari”身份访问。学校或 Jupiter 的登录记录里可能显示一台 Mac 在登录你的账号，这是 App 造成的，不是别人盗号——但如果你看到你不认识的登录，仍然应该立刻改密码。
  • App 内登录页。登录页显示在 App 里的浏览器中，而不是 Safari。技术上，任何 App 都能读取它自己内置浏览器里的内容；这个 App 的代码不读取密码栏。如果你不放心，就不要连接，手动输入成绩即可。
  • 登录状态存在手机里。谁能解锁并打开你的手机和这个 App，谁就能看到你的成绩，甚至用 App 内浏览器进入你的 Jupiter。请开启 Face ID 保护，不要把手机借给别人用这个 App。
  • 自动点击 NYCENET 按钮属于自动化操作。它不输入密码，但仍然是“机器在替你点”。
  • 后台同步由 iOS 决定时间，不保证实时，也可能消耗少量电量和流量。
  • 手机丢了怎么办：立刻在电脑上修改 NYCDOE 账号密码，这会让手机里保存的登录状态失效。

6. Google Classroom 作业：怎么运作，有哪些风险

这个版本有两种连接方式，在“作业”页点 Classroom 卡片选择：

方式 A：网页登录（默认）

  • 你在 Google 自己的登录页登录 classroom.google.com。App 把 Google 的登录 Cookie 复制一份存进钥匙串。
  • 之后 App 在看不见的内置浏览器里打开 Classroom 的“待办”页面（已分配、缺交、已上交 3 个页面），读取作业标题、课程、截止时间和链接。打开 App（距离上次至少 10 分钟）、下拉刷新时会自动读取。
  • 只读，不会提交、修改或删除 Classroom 里的任何内容。

网页登录的风险：

  • 不是 Google 提供的方式。Google 服务条款不允许用非 Google 提供的方式自动访问其服务。Google 可能要求你重新验证身份、结束登录，或者认为这是可疑活动。
  • 学校的 Google Workspace 管理员可以看到登录记录。记录里会显示“Mac 上的 Safari”。学校可能禁止或调查这类登录。
  • 登录 Cookie 存在手机里，风险和第 5 节一样：能打开你手机和 App 的人能看到你的作业，App 内浏览器也带着你的 Google 登录。
  • 截止时间是推算的。Classroom 网页不显示年份，App 会按今天的日期推算，可能差一年或差几个小时。
  • Google 改网页后读取可能失败或漏掉作业。

方式 B：Google 授权

  • 通过 Google 官方授权页，经你同意后，App 获得只读权限：课程列表、作业、你的提交状态。令牌存在钥匙串里，App 只从你的手机直接访问 Google。
  • 对 Google API 数据的使用遵守 Google API Services User Data Policy，包括 Limited Use（有限使用）要求：数据只用于在你手机上显示你的作业，不转给任何人，不用于广告。
  • 风险：这个 App 不是 Google 审核过的 App，Google 可能显示“未经验证的应用”警告；很多学校的管理员会直接阻止它，这时请改用手动添加作业或网页登录。你可以随时在 App 内“移除本机授权”，或在 myaccount.google.com/permissions 撤销。

7. 为什么不要反复点“读取成绩” / “同步”

这一节专门说清楚，因为这是最容易出问题的地方。

  • 一次读取 = 很多次访问。读取成绩时，App 会把 Jupiter 上你的每一门课、每个 Quarter 的页面挨个打开。如果你有 8 门课，一次读取就是几十次页面访问。Classroom 网页同步每次会打开 3 个页面并滚动加载。
  • 连续快速点按看起来就像攻击或抓取程序。网站可能因此限速、弹出验证、临时锁定账号，学校 IT 也可能注意到异常。
  • 不会让成绩更新得更快。老师录入成绩后 Jupiter 才会有，反复点只会读到同样的内容。
  • App 已经做的保护：两次手动同步至少间隔 1 分钟（1 分钟内再点会提示你稍后再试）；打开 App 自动同步至少间隔 10 分钟；后台刷新由 iOS 安排，大约最多每 30 分钟一次；Classroom 网页同步也至少间隔 1 分钟。
  • 正确做法：正常情况下根本不用点，打开 App 就会自动更新。读取失败时，先等几分钟，去 Jupiter 官方网页确认能否正常打开；还不行就点“重新登录”，不要连续重试。看到“登录已过期”时，自己重新登录一次即可。

8. 通知、锁屏和共享设备

  • 通知全部由手机本地发出，不经过任何服务器。新成绩通知会显示课程、作业和分数，学校通知（远程日、停课等）来自 GitHub 上的公开校历。
  • 锁屏上的通知可能被旁人看到。不想让别人看到分数，可以在 iPhone“设置 › 通知 › Student Center › 显示预览”里选“解锁时”或“从不”。
  • 实时活动和小组件会在锁屏/主屏显示今天的课程和作业。
  • 不要在借来的、共用的或学校的设备上使用这个 App 连接你的账号。

9. Face ID、相机、相册和 AI

  • Face ID / 密码完全由 iOS 处理，App 只知道“验证成功或失败”。
  • 相机和相册只在你主动扫描学生卡、课表或选择照片时使用，图片只在本机识别。
  • 作业优先级的 AI 评分使用 Apple 的本机模型（如果设备支持）或简单规则，不发送到任何服务器。

10. 未成年人

Student Center 为高中生设计，不面向 13 岁以下儿童。App 不收集任何人的个人信息。如果你未满 18 岁，建议在家长或监护人知情的情况下连接学校账号，并一起阅读第 5–7 节的风险。

11. 怎样删除你的数据

  1. 在“成绩”页点“断开 Jupiter”：删除本机读取的成绩、App 内的 Jupiter 网站数据和钥匙串里的登录 Cookie。
  2. 在“作业” › Classroom 里点“退出网页登录”：删除 Google 网站数据和钥匙串里的 Google Cookie（已导入的作业会保留，可以手动删除）。
  3. 在“作业” › Classroom 里“移除本机授权”，并可在 myaccount.google.com/permissions 撤销。
  4. 其他资料可以在 App 内逐项编辑或删除。
  5. 先做完上面几步再删除 App。iOS 有时会在删除 App 后继续保留钥匙串里的内容，所以登录状态最好在 App 内先清除。

12. 免责声明

App 按“现状”提供，不保证读取到的成绩、作业、课表和校历完整、准确或及时，也不保证与 Jupiter、Clever、NYCENET、Google 的连接一直可用。因使用 App 连接学校或 Google 账号产生的后果（包括账号受限、违反学校规定等）由使用者自行承担。所有成绩、截止时间、毕业要求请以学校和官方系统为准。

如果本政策有变化，新版本会发布在这个网址并更新日期；重大变化会在 App 内或校历通知中提示。有问题请在 GitHub Issues 留言（不要在留言里写你的密码、学号或成绩）。

5. Jupiter grades: how it works and the risks

What this edition does

  • You tap “Connect Jupiter” and sign in on Clever / NYCENET’s own pages. The app’s code doesn’t read your password.
  • After sign-in, the app copies the Jupiter, Clever and NYCENET sign-in cookies into the iOS Keychain so you stay signed in after the app is closed.
  • The app then opens Jupiter in a hidden in-app browser and reads each class’s grades and assignments: when the app opens (at least 10 minutes after the last sync), on pull-to-refresh or “Sync now”, and in iOS background refresh (roughly at most every 30 minutes, timing decided by iOS).
  • If the session has ended, the app reopens Clever and automatically taps the “NYCENET” button on Clever’s page so a still-valid school sign-in reconnects. It never types a password; if one is needed it stops and notifies you to sign in yourself.
  • For new or changed grades, the app posts a local notification showing the class, assignment and score.

Risks (please read)

  • Not authorized. Jupiter offers students no public API; the app reads the web pages you can see. Jupiter’s, Clever’s or NYC DOE’s terms and your school’s Acceptable Use Policy may not allow automated access. If they decide you broke the rules they may restrict access, lock the account or ask you to explain.
  • It can look like a bot. Each sync opens all your class pages in a row. Frequent syncs, repeated taps, or many students using it at once create unusual traffic that can trigger rate limits or security checks.
  • Grades can be wrong. If Jupiter changes its pages, reading can fail, skip classes or misread numbers. The app’s GPA is its own estimate, not an official transcript, and can’t be used for appeals or applications.
  • Browser identity. To get the desktop tables the app identifies as Safari on a Mac, so school or Jupiter sign-in records may show a Mac signing in to your account. That is the app, not an intruder — but if you see a sign-in you don’t recognize, change your password right away.
  • In-app sign-in page. The sign-in page appears inside the app’s browser, not Safari. Technically any app can read its own in-app browser; this app’s code doesn’t read password fields. If you’re not comfortable with that, don’t connect and type grades in yourself.
  • Your sign-in lives on the phone. Anyone who can unlock your phone and open the app can see your grades and even open your Jupiter in the in-app browser. Turn on Face ID protection and don’t lend the phone to others to use this app.
  • Tapping the NYCENET button automatically is an automated action. It doesn’t enter a password, but it is still the app clicking for you.
  • Background sync runs when iOS decides, isn’t real time, and uses a little battery and data.
  • If you lose your phone, change your NYCDOE password from a computer right away; that ends the sign-in stored on the phone.

6. Google Classroom: how it works and the risks

This edition has two ways to connect; choose one by tapping the Classroom card on Assignments.

Option A: Web sign-in (default)

  • You sign in to classroom.google.com on Google’s own page. The app copies Google’s sign-in cookies into the Keychain.
  • The app then opens Classroom’s To-do pages (Assigned, Missing, Done) in a hidden in-app browser and reads assignment titles, classes, due dates and links: when the app opens (at least 10 minutes after the last read) and on pull-to-refresh.
  • Read-only: it never turns in, edits or deletes anything in Classroom.

Risks of web sign-in:

  • Not a Google-provided method. Google’s Terms of Service don’t allow accessing its services by automated means other than the ones Google provides. Google may ask you to verify, end the session, or flag it as suspicious.
  • Your school’s Google Workspace admin can see sign-in records, which will show Safari on a Mac. Your school may block or look into these sign-ins.
  • Sign-in cookies live on the phone, with the same risks as section 5: anyone who can open your phone and the app can see your work, and the in-app browser carries your Google sign-in.
  • Due dates are inferred. Classroom’s pages don’t show the year, so the app guesses from today’s date and can be off by a year or a few hours.
  • If Google changes its pages, reading may fail or miss assignments.

Option B: Google authorization

  • Through Google’s official consent page, and only with your agreement, the app gets read-only access to your courses, coursework and your submission status. The token is kept in the Keychain and used only from your phone directly to Google.
  • Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements: the data is only shown to you on your phone, never shared, and never used for ads.
  • Risks: this app is not verified by Google, so Google may show an “unverified app” warning, and many schools block it outright; then add assignments yourself or use web sign-in. You can “Remove local authorization” in the app at any time or revoke it at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

7. Why you shouldn’t keep tapping “Read grades” / “Sync”

  • One read = many visits. Reading grades opens the page of every class and every quarter on Jupiter, one after another. With 8 classes that is dozens of page loads. A Classroom web sync opens 3 pages and scrolls them.
  • Rapid repeated taps look like an attack or a scraper. Sites may slow you down, show a challenge or lock the account for a while, and school IT may notice.
  • It doesn’t make grades appear sooner. Jupiter only has a grade after a teacher enters it; tapping again reads the same thing.
  • What the app already does: manual syncs are at least 1 minute apart (tapping sooner shows a reminder); automatic syncs on open are at least 10 minutes apart; background refresh is scheduled by iOS, roughly at most every 30 minutes; Classroom web syncs are also at least 1 minute apart.
  • What to do instead: normally you don’t need to tap at all — opening the app updates for you. If a read fails, wait a few minutes and check that the official Jupiter site works; if it still fails, sign in again. Don’t retry in a loop. When it says the session expired, just sign in once yourself.

8. Notifications, Lock Screen and shared devices

  • All notifications are created on the phone; no server is involved. New-grade notifications show the class, assignment and score. School notices (remote days, closures) come from the public calendar on GitHub.
  • Lock Screen notifications can be seen by people near you. To hide scores, set iPhone Settings › Notifications › Student Center › Show Previews to “When Unlocked” or “Never”.
  • The Live Activity and widgets show today’s classes and assignments on the Lock Screen and Home Screen.
  • Don’t connect your accounts in this app on a borrowed, shared or school device.

9. Face ID, camera, photos and AI

  • Face ID / passcode is handled entirely by iOS; the app only learns whether it succeeded.
  • The camera and Photos are used only when you choose to scan an ID or schedule or pick a photo, and images are processed on the device.
  • Assignment priority ratings use Apple’s on-device model (when available) or simple rules. Nothing is sent to a server.

10. Minors

Student Center is designed for high school students and is not directed to children under 13. It collects no personal information from anyone. If you are under 18, we suggest connecting school accounts with a parent or guardian aware, and reading sections 5–7 together.

11. Deleting your data

  1. On Grades, tap “Disconnect Jupiter”: removes read grades, Jupiter website data and the sign-in cookies in the Keychain.
  2. In Assignments › Classroom, tap “Sign out of web sync”: removes Google website data and the Google cookies in the Keychain (imported assignments stay; delete them yourself if you want).
  3. In Assignments › Classroom, tap “Remove local authorization”, and optionally revoke at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  4. Edit or delete anything else in the app.
  5. Do the steps above before deleting the app. iOS can keep Keychain items after an app is deleted, so clear sign-ins inside the app first.
