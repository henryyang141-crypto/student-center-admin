不要反复点“读取成绩” · Don’t keep tapping “Read grades”

每次读取都会打开 Jupiter 上你每一门课的页面。反复点按可能被当成机器人，导致限速或账号被临时锁定。
Each read opens every class page on Jupiter. Repeated taps can look like a bot and lead to rate limits or a temporary lock.
